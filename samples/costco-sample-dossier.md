## 8. What changed, in plain language

This section states only what the filings in Sections 3–7 show. It is a summary of the record, not an opinion about the company.

1. **Revenue, operating income, and net income were all higher in FY2026 than in FY2025.** Revenue went from $275.235 billion to $303.154 billion (+10.1%); operating income from $10.383 billion to $11.685 billion (+12.5%); net income from $8.099 billion to $9.226 billion (+13.9%).
   **PROOF:** FY2025 10-K, accession 0000909832-25-000101, filed 2025-10-08; FY2026 10-K, accession 0000909832-26-000093, filed 2026-10-07 (links in Section 7).
2. **The cash balance rose 42.7%, from $14.161 billion to $20.207 billion, between the two fiscal year-ends.** Total assets rose 15.5% ($77.099 billion to $89.045 billion) and stockholders' equity rose 22.8% ($29.164 billion to $35.803 billion) over the same interval.
   **PROOF:** FY2025 10-K, accession 0000909832-25-000101; FY2026 10-K, accession 0000909832-26-000093 (links in Section 7).
3. **Net income as a share of revenue moved from 2.94% to 3.04%; operating income as a share of revenue moved from 3.77% to 3.85%.** Both are arithmetic on the figures in Section 7.
   **PROOF:** Computed from the FY2025 and FY2026 10-K figures cited in Section 7.
4. **Quarterly results arrived on schedule.** Seven 8-Ks under Item 2.02 (results of operations) were filed in the period — one for each quarter — on 2025-03-06, 2025-05-29, 2025-09-25, 2025-12-11, 2026-03-05, 2026-05-28, and 2026-09-24. Five 10-Q quarterly reports were filed in the period (2025-03-13, 2025-06-05, 2025-12-17, 2026-03-11, 2026-06-03); the fourth quarter of each fiscal year has no 10-Q because the 10-K covers it.
   **PROOF:** SEC EDGAR submissions record, CIK 0000909832 — https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000909832.json — Item 2.02 8-K dates and 10-Q dates as listed in Sections 3 and 5.
5. **Officer and director changes were disclosed six times.** Six 8-Ks under Item 5.02 were filed: 2025-02-03, 2025-06-11, 2025-08-07, 2025-10-20, 2026-10-16, and 2026-11-10.
   **PROOF:** SEC EDGAR submissions record, CIK 0000909832 — https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000909832.json — Item 5.02 8-Ks listed in Section 5.
6. **Shareholders voted twice.** Item 5.07 8-Ks (matters submitted to a vote of security holders) were filed on 2025-01-27 and 2026-01-21, after the annual meetings. The proxy statement (DEF 14A), additional proxy materials (DEFA14A), and annual report to security holders (ARS) for the FY2025 year were all filed on 2025-12-04. Two shareholder-proposal notices (PX14A6G) were filed on 2025-01-17.
